WhatsApp group pictures

One picture for each BHD and CupsByAA WhatsApp group. The colour says the kind of group: green and gold for finance, coral for production, plum for CupsByAA, riso for cup suppliers, navy and orange for partners. The brand pictures are here. Every one is 1080 × 1080, laid out for a circular crop, and checked at 40, 64 and 128 px before it was kept.

Setting one on WhatsApp

Download the PNG, then in WhatsApp open Settings, tap your photo, choose Gallery or Photos, pick the file and confirm without zooming. The art is already positioned for the circle, so any crop you add will eat into the breathing room around the mark.

Built on the official mark from the BHD brand assets. Colours are the canonical trio: cyan #009bc1, yellow #ffbb00, purple #824598. Part of design.bhd.om.
