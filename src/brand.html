Rules

ColourCyan on light or dark. Never recolour, never gradient, never add a shadow.
Clear spaceKeep clear space equal to the height of the mark's stem on all four sides.
Minimum size16px for a favicon, 24px anywhere else. Below that use the wordmark instead.
Do notStretch, rotate, outline, or place on a busy photograph.

The hard Arabic rule

للرخام

Correct. No letter-spacing, the cursive join holds.

للرخام

Wrong. Tracking breaks the join and reads as illiterate. For emphasis use kashida (U+0640), never tracking.

tokens.css enforces this with a global rule on :lang(ar), [dir="rtl"] and .ar.
